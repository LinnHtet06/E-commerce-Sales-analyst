# E-commerce Sales Analysis and Decision Tree Modelling

## Project Overview
This academic Business Intelligence project analyses the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset, containing over 100,000 orders from 2016 to 2018. The project explores sales trends, customer purchasing behaviour, product categories, payment preferences and delivery performance using data visualisation and data mining techniques.

## Objectives
- Analyse monthly revenue trends and seasonal patterns.
- Explore sales performance and customer distribution across Brazilian states.
- Identify top-performing product categories and payment preferences.
- Investigate delivery performance and the relationship between product prices and freight costs.
- Apply a J48 decision tree to explore product-category purchasing patterns.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Tableau:** Data visualisation and business intelligence dashboards.
- **Weka:** Data mining and J48 decision-tree modelling.
- **Data preparation:** Missing-value handling, duplicate removal, outlier treatment and class-imbalance handling.

## Key Findings
- Sales showed seasonal peaks around November and December.
- Sales and customer activity were concentrated in southeastern Brazil.
- Bed/bath/table was a leading product category.
- Credit cards represented the largest payment value in the analysed data.
- Delivery performance varied by state, highlighting potential logistics challenges in remote regions.
- The J48 decision-tree model achieved 97.8% accuracy and 98.9% recall for the minority class, as reported in the project presentation.

## Business Recommendations
- Plan inventory around seasonal sales patterns.
- Explore targeted marketing based on geographic and product-category trends.
- Investigate delivery delays and freight-cost inefficiencies.
- Explore cross-selling opportunities using product-purchasing patterns.
